# Open questions and records requests — 2026-09-10

Ordered by how directly a document would settle them. Packet-dependent items are deferred until the packet is retrieved.

## Ask on the record tonight (news-connected; no packet needed)

1. **How many of the reassigned special-education students had an ARD/IEP meeting with their family before they changed campuses on Aug 10, how many have had one since, and how many are still waiting?**
   Would be answered by: HISD's monthly progress reports to TEA under the July 7 letter (August and September submissions), and the ARD-meeting log the conservators would need to certify.
2. **Will the district post its monthly TEA progress reports and the October corrective-action report publicly, before Oct 9?**
   Answered by: the reports themselves. Request under the Public Information Act if not volunteered.
3. **What is the current status of the OCR investigation opened in May: has the district received a data request, and has it responded?**
   Answered by: OCR's notification letter and HISD's response.
4. **Which board action authorized ending the Grimes Park lease, and which authorized the Southside Launchpad project and its funding source?**
   Answered by: the board item(s) and minutes; the termination notice to the City (with date); the project budget and fund (bond, fund balance, other).
5. **How many campuses have a certified librarian on staff in 2026-27, by campus, and how does that compare to 2025-26?**
   Answered by: PEIMS staff roster extract by campus and role.
6. **What share of teachers of record are uncertified as of the fall snapshot, by NES/non-NES status?**
   Answered by: PEIMS fall snapshot; Texas Monthly's ">20%" figure needs a denominator.
7. **Which 33 campuses are not on automatic free meals this year, and what changed in their eligibility?**
   Answered by: the district's CEP election list submitted to TDA.

## Packet-dependent (run once the packet is in)

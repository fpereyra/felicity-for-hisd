## Item 8 — FOC(LOCAL), Student Discipline: Placement in a DAEP — CREATION (pp93–95; `FOC-094.png`, `FOC-095.png`)
Entire text is new (all underlined). No prior LOCAL policy existed.

| Section | New text (summary, close to verbatim) |
|---|---|
| Appeal | After a DAEP placement decision under FOC(LEGAL) and the Student Code of Conduct (SCOC), a parent may appeal "in accordance with the SCOC." |
| Days | District business days; day received = day zero. |
| Appeal Request | Written, on the District form, to the **director of the Student Discipline department**, within **five** business days after the DAEP placement conference. Untimely appeal "may" be dismissed. |
| Appeal Conference | Director or designee schedules within three business days; may proceed and decide in the parent's absence. |
| Review / Decision | Director may consider conference and appeal information, SCOC, law, procedures, "any other relevant information." Written decision within two business days; may uphold, modify, overturn. **"The determination of the director of the Student Discipline department or designee is final."** |
| Placement status | Placement **remains in effect pending the appeal**. |
| General provisions | "Shall not be construed to create new or additional rights beyond those granted by law, Board policy, or the SCOC." |

Five questions:
- Objective standard/threshold deleted: none (new policy). It **sets** a 5-day filing window and 3+2-day district clock.
- Board reporting deleted: none. Note: **no appeal to the Board or to any official outside the Student Discipline department**; contrast FOD below.
- Parent/student right deleted: cannot be determined from the packet. The prior route lived in the SCOC, which is not in the packet. If the SCOC previously allowed a second-level (e.g., hearing officer or chief) review, this policy narrows it. **Request the current SCOC DAEP appeal section.**
- Delegation to non-packet regulation: yes, "in accordance with the SCOC" and "District procedures."
- Self-error detection: the deciding official is the head of the department whose staff made the placement; no independent check.

## Item 9 — FOD(LOCAL), Student Discipline: Expulsion — CREATION (pp96–98; `FOD-097.png`, `FOD-098.png`)
Entire text new.
- Level 1: written appeal to a **"designated District official"** within five business days of the written expulsion order; conference within three days; written decision within two days; may uphold/modify/overturn.
- Level 2: **Appeal to the Board** within five business days of the written decision; designated official prepares the administrative record and places it on a Board hearings agenda; Board may consider the record and "any additional information it determines appropriate"; Board may uphold/modify/overturn; **"The decision of the Board is final."**
- Expulsion remains in effect pending final disposition "unless otherwise required by law or directed by the Board."
- Same "no new or additional rights" clause.
Five questions: no deletions (new). Board retains the final say on expulsions (consistent with TEC 37.009(f)). Delegation: "District governance procedures" for placing hearings on the agenda.
